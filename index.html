Twelve cards on the table. Five in the dealer's hand. Call it.

How to play
  1. The dealer is holding five of the twelve cards on the table, and admits what the hand ranks as.
  2. Pick five cards to call the hand. Each card comes back with a tell — right card, right place, in the hand, wrong place, or not in the hand.
  3. You get four calls: the flop, the turn, the river and the showdown.
  • The hand reads left to right: the cards that make the rank first, then the spares — highest first, ties settled by suit (♠ ♥ ♦ ♣).
  • Each call also shows what your own five cards would rank as.
  • A fresh deal at midnight.
